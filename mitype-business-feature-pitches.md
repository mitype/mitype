# Small Business Profiles — Marketing Copy

## For Small Business Owners — 4 Reasons to Set Up Your Profile

1. **Reach hyper-local customers in your exact ZIP code who are already scrolling Mitype** — your next regular might be the next member who joins down the street, and they'll find you the moment they tap "Looking for a local small business?" on Discover.

2. **Get free, daily spotlight exposure** — Mitype rotates one small business into a featured hero card on every member's Discover page, every single day, with zero ad spend required.

3. **Skip the social-media maze** — your logo, services, hours, contact info, and upcoming events all sit on one clean profile that members can tap-to-call, tap-to-message, or open in Maps with one finger.

4. **Build a real customer relationship channel** — every member who taps Save pins your business to the purple Small Business Saves tab in their Messages center, and our end-to-end encrypted DMs mean you can talk about pricing, bookings, and orders without any third party reading along.


## For Mitype Members — 3 Things the New Feature Does for You

1. **A "Looking for a local small business?" tab on Discover** — tap once and you see every published business in your ZIP code, filterable by type (Salon, Plumber, Coffee Shop, Lash Tech, Notary, and 50+ more) with one-tap View and Message buttons on every card.

2. **A Business Profile under any creator's profile** — whenever someone you discover also runs a business, a purple "🏪 View Business" pill shows up next to their Share button, and one tap takes you to their logo, services, contact info, and upcoming events.

3. **A Small Business Saves tab at the top of your Messages** — tap the ☆ Save on any business profile and it pins to a dedicated purple tab in your Messages sidebar, so the salon you wanted to try, the mechanic you trust, and the chef you've been meaning to book are always one tap away.
