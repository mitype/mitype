# Mitype social drafts: videos and captions

Four vertical videos (1080x1920, silent) are in `marketing/videos`. Add trending or licensed audio inside TikTok, Instagram or Facebook when you post, since each app has its own free music library.

Link note: put `mitypeapp.com` in your bio and tell people "link in bio" on Instagram and TikTok, because captions there are not clickable. Facebook captions can carry the link directly.

Referral note: if your own link is a referral link, use `mitypeapp.com/?ref=yourusername` in the Facebook caption and the bio.
