## Posting strategy (free tips)

- **Best times to post:** Tuesday–Thursday between 12–3 PM, and Sunday evenings (7–9 PM) — when adults are scrolling and thinking about the week.
- **Rotate the hook style.** Don't post the boredom angle three times in a row. Mix question / list / story / direct.
- **Pair with images.** Posts with your branded social ad image (the one I generated as `02_ad_square_instagram.png`) get more reach than text-only.
- **Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes.** Facebook's algorithm rewards posts with early engagement.
- **Boost the top performers.** Watch which 2–3 posts get the most organic engagement, then put $20 behind those as boosted posts. Way more ROI than guessing what'll work.
- **Always end on the URL line by itself.** Facebook's preview card needs a clean URL on its own line to render properly.
