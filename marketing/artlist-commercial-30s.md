## Production overview

This commercial follows one Hispanic woman across two warm, lived-in spaces — a sun-drenched coffee shop and a friend's beautifully appointed living room — and ends on a clean brand card with QR fade-in. The whole piece feels like a real DTC commercial you'd see during a streaming ad break.

**Workflow before you start:**

1. Generate **one hero reference still** of the lead woman first (prompt below). Approve the still — this becomes your character reference.
2. Use **Image-to-Video** with that reference for every scene she's in, so her face stays consistent across cuts.
3. Generate audio OFF on every clip. We'll add the dialogue and voice-over in your editor.
4. Assemble in DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, or Final Cut. Drop the SFX and music from Artlist's library underneath.
